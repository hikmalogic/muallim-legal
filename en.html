Muallim — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026 · Revised October 3, 2026: section 5.

Muallim is a mobile app for practicing spoken Modern Standard Arabic (fusha) with an AI teacher. The app is developed and maintained by an individual developer ("we", "the developer"). This page describes what data the app processes and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

DataPurposeWhere it is stored
Email address and password Account registration and sign-in (authentication) Supabase (the password is stored only as a cryptographic hash)
Display name (optional) and selected language level Lesson personalization Supabase
Your voice during conversations with the AI teacher Real-time speech recognition and the teacher's reply Streamed to processing providers in real time (see section 2); we do not store audio recordings
Text transcripts of conversations and learning progress Lesson history, progress tracking, better personalization Supabase

2. Voice processing by third-party providers

Voice dialogue is the core of the app. When you speak with the AI teacher, your voice audio is streamed in real time to the following providers:

  • Deepgram — speech recognition (voice → text);
  • Google Gemini — generation of the teacher's reply (text → text);
  • ElevenLabs and/or Microsoft Azure — the teacher's speech synthesis (text → voice).

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. We send them only what is required for the dialogue to work, and we never share your email or other account data with them.

3. Where data is stored

Accounts, transcripts and learning progress are stored in a Supabase database hosted in the Singapore region. All data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.
  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not collect any data beyond what is listed in section 1.

5. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and associated data right in the app: the «Профиль» (Profile) tab → «Удалить аккаунт…» (Delete account…); your password and a confirmation word are required, and deletion happens immediately. Without the app, email the contact address below from the address your account is registered with: we will send a confirmation code to the account address and delete the data no later than 30 days after your reply with the code. What is deleted, what is kept and why — see the Account deletion page.

6. Children

The app is not intended for unsupervised use by children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Material changes will additionally be announced in the app.

8. Contact

For privacy questions and data deletion requests: hikmalogic@gmail.com
