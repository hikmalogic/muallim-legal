Account and data deletion

The “Muallim — учитель арабского” app on Google Play and “Mudarris” on the App Store · developer hikmalogic (individual developer) · revised October 3, 2026

1. Delete in the app

The app interface is in Russian; button labels are given in quotes with a translation.

  1. Open the «Профиль» (Profile) tab and tap «Удалить аккаунт…» (Delete account…).
  2. Read what will be deleted and what will be kept.
  3. Enter your account password and the word УДАЛИТЬ (DELETE).
  4. Tap «Удалить аккаунт навсегда» (Delete account permanently).

Your account and data are deleted immediately; the app shows «Аккаунт удалён» (Account deleted) and returns to the sign-in screen. A deleted account cannot be restored.

If the connection drops during deletion, the app tells you that the result is unknown. Repeat the deletion once you are back online — repeating is safe; the app will also re-check the result on its next launch. If you can no longer sign in to the account, it has been deleted. If the app cannot check the result, clear the app’s data in your phone settings (or delete the app) — this also erases the progress saved on the device.

2. Delete without the app — by email

This also works if you do not remember your password.

  1. Email hikmalogic@gmail.com from the address your account is registered with, subject “Account deletion”.
  2. We will reply to the account address with a confirmation code. Send it back in your reply — this proves the request comes from the owner of the address. We do not accept requests sent from other addresses: please write from your account address.
  3. We will delete the account the same way the app does, no later than 30 days after your email with the code, and confirm the deletion by reply.

We keep deletion correspondence for no longer than 30 days after our reply.

3. What is deleted

  • the account: email address, password (stored only as a cryptographic hash), display name and language level;
  • learning and vocabulary progress;
  • exercise answers, transcripts of voice lessons with the teacher, and the teacher’s notes about you;
  • your reports about errors in the learning materials;
  • minutes: the balance, top-ups and session history — without a refund;
  • records of activation code attempts made with your address (the address is stored there as a hash);
  • sign-in records in the authentication service log;
  • on the device where you deleted the account — saved progress and the signed-in session.

4. What is kept and why

  • Redeemed activation codes stay redeemed so that they cannot be entered again; their link to you is removed.
  • An anonymized record that a deletion happened: the date, the method (in the app or by email) and counts of what was deleted — with no address or account number.
  • Other devices where you signed in, and device backups (Google, iCloud), keep a copy of the progress until you delete the app or its data there. Sign-in on those devices stops working.
  • Logs of the voice teacher server (they may contain lesson utterances with the account number, but no email address) are not cleared immediately: they are overwritten as they fill up.
  • Technical logs of the hosting platform (Supabase) are kept for the period set by the provider. We do not make separate database backups.

5. What was shared with services and stays with them

  • LiveKit (voice connection): during a conversation, audio and the text of utterances pass through it in real time; the account number, room names and session metadata, including the teacher’s notes, stay with LiveKit under its rules.
  • Speech recognition and synthesis services (Deepgram, Microsoft Azure, ElevenLabs; Groq if speech recognition through it is enabled), pronunciation assessment (Microsoft Azure — voice recordings from exercises), the teacher’s replies and exercise checking (Google Gemini — utterances, exercise answers, the teacher’s notes) — under those services’ rules. Your email address is never sent to them. During a conversation, audio is streamed in real time.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

If you bought minutes or a subscription through the App Store, Google Play or a payment service (for example, an activation code), the purchase history is kept by them under their rules — we cannot delete it. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play settings.

7. Contact

Questions about deletion and data: hikmalogic@gmail.com. How we process data — see the privacy policy.
